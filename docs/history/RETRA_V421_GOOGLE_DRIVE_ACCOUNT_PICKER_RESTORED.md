# Retra v4.21 — Google Drive Account Picker Restored

- Restores the native Android Google account chooser on first-time Google Drive sync.
- The chooser is filtered to Google accounts already signed in on the device (`com.google`).
- After selecting an account, Retra opens the Android Drive folder picker to authorize the Retra save folder.
- Retra remembers and displays the selected Google account in Settings.
- Sync settings include Sync now, Change Google account, Change Drive folder, and Disconnect.
- Disconnect clears both the stored Drive folder and selected account.
- Preserves all v4.20 Remote Link reliability and native settings work.
